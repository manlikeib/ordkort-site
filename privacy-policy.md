# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 August 2026

Ordkort is a Swedish vocabulary-learning application developed by Ibrahim
Jimoh.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the
Ordkort mobile application.

## Summary

Ordkort is designed to work without requiring an account.

The current version of Ordkort:

- does not require registration or login
- does not collect personal information
- does not use advertising
- does not use analytics or tracking services
- does not contain in-app purchases
- does not sell or share user information
- stores learning progress and preferences locally on the user's device

## Information stored on the device

Ordkort stores information needed to provide its learning features locally
on the user's device.

This may include:

- study progress
- review history
- favorite words
- words marked as difficult
- daily study goals
- onboarding status
- active study-session information
- app preferences

This information is used only to provide the app's functionality.

The information is not transmitted to the developer or stored on an Ordkort
server.

## Personal information

Ordkort does not require users to provide personal information such as:

- name
- email address
- phone number
- postal address
- date of birth
- account credentials

Ordkort does not create user accounts.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Ordkort does not currently use:

- analytics services
- advertising networks
- tracking technologies
- cross-app tracking
- personalized advertising
- advertising identifiers

## Sharing of information

Ordkort does not sell, rent, trade, or share user information with third
parties.

Because the app does not transmit locally stored learning data to the
developer, the developer does not have access to that data.

## Data retention and deletion

Learning progress and preferences remain stored locally on the device until
they are removed by the user or the app is uninstalled.

Uninstalling Ordkort normally removes the app's locally stored data. Device
backups or operating-system services may retain copies according to the
user's device and backup settings.

## Children's privacy

Ordkort does not knowingly collect personal information from children or
other users.

The app provides general educational vocabulary content and does not require
users to submit personal information.

## Third-party services

Ordkort is built using Expo and React Native technologies.

The current version does not intentionally integrate analytics,
advertising, account-management, payment, or social-media services.

The app may rely on operating-system and app-store services provided by
Apple or Google for downloading, installing, updating, securing, and
distributing the application. Those services are governed by the privacy
policies of Apple and Google.

## Security

Ordkort minimizes privacy risk by keeping learning information locally on
the user's device and by not requiring a user account.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices using the
security features provided by their operating system.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Ordkort's features or data-handling
practices change.

Any updated version will include a revised effective date and will be made
available through the app's public privacy-policy page.
